JuggleChat Privacy Policy

Updated: July 28, 2026
Effective: July 28, 2026

JuggleChat (the “App,” “we,” or “us”) values your personal information and privacy. This Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when the App provides messaging and related features, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Account and Profile Information

When you register, sign in, or maintain an account, we may process your email or other login identifier, verification codes, encrypted authentication information, and profile information you choose to provide, such as nickname, avatar, account identifier, and bio.

1.2 Communications and Social Content

To provide direct and group messaging, synchronization, favorites, social posts, and calls, we process text, images, audio, video, voice messages, files, stickers, group information, interactions, and necessary message metadata such as sender, recipient, time, and delivery status.

1.3 Device, Network, and Log Information

For connectivity, security, and troubleshooting, we may process device model, operating-system and App versions, network status, IP address, connection and crash logs, operation time, error codes, and pseudonymous technical identifiers. Passwords and verification codes are not written to application logs.

1.4 Device Permissions

  • Camera: QR scanning, taking photos, sending images, and video calls.
  • Microphone: voice messages and audio/video calls, accessed only when you actively use those features.
  • Photos and files: selecting, sending, saving, or previewing avatars, images, videos, and files.
  • Notifications: new-message, incoming-call, and service-status alerts.

You decide whether to grant these permissions and may revoke them in system settings. Revocation affects only features that require the permission.

2. Why We Use Information

We process information only as necessary to:

  • create and manage accounts, verify identity, and maintain sessions;
  • send, receive, synchronize, store, and display messages, groups, contacts, and social content;
  • provide calls, QR scanning, media selection, notifications, and feedback;
  • protect accounts, networks, and services against fraud, abuse, and attacks;
  • diagnose failures and improve performance and user experience; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. To deliver the service, the App may transmit the information necessary for a feature to the application backend, JuggleIM messaging services, audio/video services, object storage, or notification services. Such providers should process information only for the agreed purpose and to the extent necessary.

We otherwise share, transfer, or publicly disclose personal information only with your separate consent, to complete a feature you request, as part of a lawful merger or asset transfer, or where required by applicable law or a competent authority.

4. Retention and Security

Storage location and retention depend on the server deployment you use and applicable law. Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes in this Policy and legal obligations, and is then deleted or anonymized.

We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls, transport protection, encrypted password storage, least privilege, and security logging. No internet service is completely secure. If a security incident occurs, we will take legally required remedial steps and notify affected users where required.

5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may access, correct, or delete profile information and content; withdraw device permissions or consent; request account deletion; and ask questions, object, or complain about processing. These options are available through profile settings, system settings, and the in-app Feedback feature.

Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed before withdrawal. Deleting an account or information required for the service may prevent some or all features from operating.

6. Children

Minors should use the App only with the guidance and consent of a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that a minor’s personal information was processed without appropriate guardian consent, we will delete it or take other protective action as required by law.

7. Policy Updates

This Policy may change as features, laws, or data practices evolve. Material changes will be communicated reasonably, such as through an in-app notice. If you do not agree with an updated Policy, stop using the affected service.

8. Contact

Questions, complaints, or requests concerning personal information may be submitted through the App’s Feedback feature.
